# Music Discovery

Listeners explore music related to a song they recently played or its artist. They can open suggested songs and artists in Apple Music.

## Language

**Recently Played**:
The collection of songs available from the listener’s recent Apple Music activity.
_Avoid_ — Listening history, play log

**Discovery Seed**:
The recently played song or its artist that the listener chooses as the starting point for finding similar music.

**Discovery Mode**:
The kind of similarity the listener chooses to explore. Similar Songs starts from the selected song, and Similar Artists starts from its artist.

**Recommendation**:
A song or artist suggested for a Discovery Seed of the same kind.

**Catalog Match**:
The confidently identified Apple Music listing for a Recommendation that the listener can open from their catalog.
